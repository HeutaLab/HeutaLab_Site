Our apps

Two apps, built while teaching.

Both run in the browser, both keep your class data on your own device, and both were written for the jobs that eat a teacher's week — recording what children can actually do, and running the screen at the front of the room.

C Creance

Assessment recorded while
you teach, not after.

Paste in a class list, add your own success criteria, and mark against them as you move around the room. Every child's picture builds up criterion by criterion, so the gaps show themselves before the end of the unit rather than after it.

  • Mark as Not started, Developing, Secure or Mastered — in Grid, Focus or Tap mode, the last built for marking from a phone on the move.
  • High achiever and encouragement flags appear on their own, next to the children they apply to.
  • Class breakdown and analytics show how the whole group is doing on a topic at a glance.
  • Export the lot as a spreadsheet when reports are due.
  • Names, notes and photos stay on the device behind a PIN — back everything up to a single file.
Open the example →

Opens on the class shown here — two invented classes and a term of marks, so there is something to look at. Your school's own copy lives at creance.heutalab.com, starts empty, and keeps its data separate from anything tried out here.

S Sage on the Stage

The screen at the front
of the room.

Around fifty widgets — timers, name picker, group maker, traffic light, draw pad, teaching clock, noise meter — that you drop onto a screen and arrange however the lesson needs. Build a deck ahead of time, or add a tool the moment you need it.

  • Screens you can prepare as a deck, so a lesson's tools are one tap apart.
  • Paste a class register once and every name tool uses it.
  • Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — the noise meter and webcam included.
  • Every widget is documented, with a shareable link straight to any one of them.
