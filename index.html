Our apps

Six apps, built while teaching.

Five run in the browser and keep their data on the device they are opened on; the sixth is a Mac app, and what it shows never leaves the Mac. Three were written for the jobs that eat a teacher's week — recording what children can actually do, running the screen at the front of the room, and filling in the planning format. Two are handed to the children instead, and one puts the whole class's iPads on the board. Every one of them shows you something already working, so there is something to look at before you commit a class to it.

C Creance

Assessment recorded while you teach, not after.

Paste in a class list, add your own success criteria, and mark against them as you move around the room.

For the teacher Open the example →

S Sage on the Stage

The screen at the front of the room.

Around fifty widgets — timers, name picker, group maker, traffic light, draw pad — arranged however the lesson needs.

For the teacher Try it →

P PlanBench

Your school’s planning format, as an app.

The nine headings you already fill in each term, with the resources clipped to the week and the finished plan out the other end as Word.

For the teacher Open PlanBench →

L LoopLab

Children write real code, and hear whether it works.

Six levels from a first note to two loops running at once — then a club where the tracks come back with bugs to be found by ear.

For the children · 9–13 Open LoopLab →

P Perkush

A drum kit made out of whatever is in the room.

Record a clap, a stomp or a silly noise with the camera, drop it on a pad, and play your own band.

For the children Open Perkush →

F Flect

The whole class’s iPads, on one screen.

Children mirror to the teacher’s Mac the way they would to an Apple TV — up to twenty-four side by side, and click one to fill the screen.

How it plays out

One class, one year, four moments.

Lesson 001

Mark on the move

A class list and your own success criteria. Marks go in while you are still walking the room.

Week 006

The gaps surface

Enough marks to see the pattern — the criterion half the class has not met is now obvious.

Term 002

The picture holds

Class breakdown and analytics across topics, so the conversation with the leadership team has evidence in it.

Reports week

Export and go home

The whole year comes out as a spreadsheet. Nothing to reconstruct from memory.
