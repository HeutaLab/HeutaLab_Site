Minecraft Competition: The Most Marvellous Hole-in-One

See the Introductory Video Above.

Minecraft Competition

This event is through FOBISIA and Bangkok Patana school with the invitation going out to non-member schools too.

The competition is a build style for children 8-13 in three categories.

Dates run from September 5th till October 14th where we will view submissions

Prizes

We are hoping for a large bag of Minecoins to be for 1st prize and Minecraft Education badged merch such as hoodies and T-shirts. To be confirmed.

Rules

There are only a few rules in this competition to keep it as open and creative as possible.

The main rule being that your ‘ball’ must travel in three distinct phases: ‘Tee-off’, ‘The Drive’ and ‘The Putt’ with the ‘Drive’ being as complicated and as marvellous as your team can muster.

FAQs

Hit the ‘learn more’ link below for the list of FAQ and help.

Scoring

Scoring is a community event. All teams will be assigned another school or team’s world (video if errors occur) to score.

Plain ‘Drives’, short ‘Putts’ and inaccurate ‘Tee-offs’ will lead to low scores. The most inventive, accurate and lengthy are going to score the best.
