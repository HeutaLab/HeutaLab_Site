Keeping your key safe

  • Your key is saved in this browser only. The desk sends it straight to the AI service you picked; it never goes to heutalab.com.
  • On a shared or school computer, press Forget this key when you finish.
  • Set a monthly spending limit in your AI account, and never share the key or paste it into a document.
  • If you think someone else has your key, delete it in your AI account and make a new one.
Your key and what you type at the desk go from this browser to the AI service you chose (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic) under that service’s terms. Neither ever reaches heutalab.com. The page only tells heutalab.com that a request happened: the step, the service, the choices you picked, whether it worked and how long it took.
