The five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Look closely

    Study a picture and notice what is really there.

    Study the evidence
  2. 2

    Step 2: Choose and describe

    Pick a character, a place and a palette. Put what you see into words.

    Build the case file

    Step 3: Create with AI

    Get your brief here. Make the picture in your AI image tool.

    Send the brief
  4. 4

    Step 4: Check and improve

    Put the picture next to what you asked for. Change one thing.

    Compare the evidence
  5. 5

    Step 5: Build and share

    Turn your pictures into a comic page. Download or print it.
