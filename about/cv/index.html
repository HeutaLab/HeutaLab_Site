Profile

Leading Educational Technology for over 20 years.

I am a forward thinking and conscientious leader of educational technology, passionate about my subject in both my personal and professional life. Always looking for new and inventive ideas to teach, share and inspire others, as these are my core values.

I love what I do, and this gives me the confidence to assure my colleagues and students that I care about their learning and technological goals. With this background, I am committed to creative and innovative teaching, aiming to foster a lifelong love of technology for learners.

Currently Whole School Advisor for Technology at Bangkok Patana School, and the teacher behind HeutaLab — four classroom apps and fifteen years of writing for teachers.
