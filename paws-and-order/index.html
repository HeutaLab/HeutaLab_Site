The five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Look closely

    Study a picture and notice what is really there.

    On Paws & Order
  2. 2

    Step 2: Choose and describe

    Pick a character or a place, your colours and a level. Then put it into exact words.

    On Paws & Order
  3. 3

    Step 3: Create with AI

    Get your prompt here. Then make the picture in your image tool.

    In your image tool
  4. 4

    Step 4: Check and improve

    Compare what you asked for with what you got. Change one thing and try again.

    On Paws & Order
  5. 5

    Step 5: Build and share

    Put your pictures into a comic. Save it, print it or download it.

    On Paws & Order

Paws & Order helps you with the words. Your picture is made in a separate image tool, one your teacher or grown-up has said yes to.

What you will learn

  • Close observation

    Look hard at a picture. Say what is really there.

  • Precise description

    Pick exact words, so your picture comes out the way you planned.

  • Visual storytelling

    Put pictures and words in order to tell a story.

  • AI literacy

    Check what the AI made. Spot what went wrong, then change one thing.
