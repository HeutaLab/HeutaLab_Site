# Paws & Order: Gemini prompt pack

How to use: open a new Gemini chat, paste ONE prompt at a time, and keep going in the same chat so the style holds. Pick a favourite result, then upload it back into Gemini with "draw the same character in [new scene]" for later pictures.

## 0. The house style (paste it first, or let each prompt carry it)

Hand-drawn comic-book illustration that looks as if it was made by a clever, cheeky primary-school kid: thick, bold, slightly wobbly hand-inked black outlines on every character and main prop, faint visible pencil sketch lines under the ink, uneven line thickness, flat bright saturated colours (cyan blue, fiery orange, vivid green, bold purple, sunshine yellow, hot pink) with no gradients, simple coarse halftone dot patterns for shadows, a warm cream paper texture and a slight off-register print look. Flat, direct, eye-level staging with no depth of field, no realistic lighting, and nothing that looks like a photo or 3D render. Silly, friendly and safe for children aged 7 to 11.

This must be a brand-new original design. Do not copy or resemble any existing cartoon, comic, film or game character, and use no logos or brand names. Nothing scary or realistic, no weapons, and no text in the picture except what I ask for.
