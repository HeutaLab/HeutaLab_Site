Photography and learning about photos in the Primary School.

3 March 2010 · imaginglight paintingphotos

 

The Clubs that we run at school vary depending on the availabilty of the developer chemicals for the dark room. That said, photography has so many facets that we can invent something from the kit in the science rooms! This time we managed to collect a few pieces of coloured cellophane and some pen-lights (also some fabulous multi-coloured LED torches at the local market for about a $1 a piece).

The Video is in 2 parts. The 1st being the Computer imagery club we had in 2008/9 using the tutorials the children found in at Getpaint.net. They then voted on which ones they thought were the best and taught themselves the tools. Remarkable.

Watch the video to see it all.
