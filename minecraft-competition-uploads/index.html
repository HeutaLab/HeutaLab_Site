Minecraft Competition Uploads.

You need both items to submit an entry. Entries will be marked in the week ending 28th October 2022

1. Screencast

There must be a screen cast of the game.

TITLE FORMAT: TEAM NAME, SCHOOL, AGE RANGE, TEAM FIRST NAMES.

MAXIMUM LENGTH: 2 MINUTES.

IT MUST SHOW A WORKING TEE-OFF, DRIVE AND PUTT IN ONE MOTION.

YouTube links are best or good accessible online platforms with viewable panels in the page.

If Google Drive or other similar file storage, please test the rendering so teams can view in-stream and not have to download.

Test too, that the link is public.

2. Upload link to the whole .MCWorld File

TITLE FORMAT: TEAM NAME, SCHOOL, AGE RANGE, TEAM FIRST NAMES.

Google Drive or OneDrive Links keep the files one .zip-like file.

iCloud links are also good.

TEST THE FILE IS OPEN FOR PUBLIC ACCESS.
