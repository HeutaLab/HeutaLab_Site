DIY iPad Trolley for School-Like Bretford Mobility.

Before we went out and bought the iPad trolley (the tool trolley you see below) I was most concerned with the everyday use of the iPAd with the children. The Early years children would b the first to use them and no doubt be the first to drop them too!

So, I searched high and low for a suitably cheap and robust ipad case. The few that I found were either to expensive (aren't they all?) or painfully scant on the child friendly side. I came across the Kensington Rubber Protective Band, which, for all intent and purposes, fits the bill perfectly.

It's worith mentioning that Kensington also make a battery boost for the iPad (that'll come in handy later) witha stand and micro USB support.
